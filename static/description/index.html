Central register

Store the holder, document type, authority, number, issue and expiry dates, official file, responsible owner, tags, notes, and company.

Policy-driven reminders

Configure multiple thresholds, activities, email delivery, responsible owners, escalation users, and specific recipients by document type.

Controlled renewals

Move documents through draft, active, renewal, superseded, and cancelled stages while retaining the full renewal chain.

Audit-ready history

Keep immutable reminder logs with expiry snapshots, recipients, thresholds, channels, activities, and queued emails.

Evidence controls

Require document numbers, issue dates, expiry dates, and official attachments before activation according to each policy.

Secure by design
